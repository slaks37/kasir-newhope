# Data inventory and retention matrix — owner/counsel review required

Observed repository and read-only database catalog, 12 October 2026. Durations labelled **proposed** are not existing enforced retention. No purge was scheduled or performed. Merchant is controller of customer/staff POS data; New Hope generally processes that data on merchant instructions, while independently controlling account, security and subscription processing. Validate those roles contractually.

| Data / location | Purpose and candidate legal basis | Access / recipients | Retention and deletion treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase Auth identity, email, password verifier, sessions | Account contract, authentication/security; not blanket consent | Auth, verified owner, narrowly scoped internal recovery provider | Active account + approved closure/legal hold period; Auth session/refresh token TTL and backup TTL **verify dashboard**; revoke sessions on closure |
| internal tenants/merchants/outlets, memberships; public legacy mirrors | Tenant routing, merchant contract and staff administration | Verified owning business and internal authorized support | Active service; remove optional profile fields after verified closure, retaining required business/financial references; reconcile legacy mirrors |
| pos transactions/items/payments/refunds/cash/shift/audit, billing invoices/payment events | Merchant accounting, subscription contract, applicable statutory retention and disputes | Owning business, scoped financial reporting, DOKU for subscription only | Counsel assess Indonesian accounting/tax records generally requiring 10 years in Indonesia (KUP); preserve minimum required financial record and legal holds, remove unnecessary contact identifiers separately; never cascade-delete tenant/customer into ledger |
| pos customers, loyalty, vehicles and sector job/context tables | Merchant-selected customer service; evaluate necessity and controller's basis | Owning business; no raw customer context to paid LLM | **Proposed** active relationship + 12 months; controller approval and applicable exceptions required; unlink/pseudonymize optional identities while retaining required records |
| staff, commissions, payroll, attendance/geolocation | Merchant employment/admin obligations, not employee blanket consent | Owning business and limited labor administration | Applicable employment/accounting retention **legal verify**; precise attendance location must be minimized and configurable; no AI export of staff/location |
| Local browser offline state/outboxes/photos | Offline POS and sync | Device users with local access; cloud on sync | Until successful sync/merchant-approved device cleanup; unacknowledged commands/recovery history must not be silently lost. Device handover/export deletion checklist required; browser clearing is not cloud erasure |
| AI request audit and credit wallet | Cost, debugging, security and invoice support | Owner audit, limited backend; future query_text stores hash, not plaintext | **Proposed** 90-day operational AI audit; retain minimum charge/consent evidence as legally needed. Historical plaintext requires a reviewed backfill/purge; none applied. Hashes/pseudonyms are not proven anonymization |
| AI consent events and legal notice acknowledgements | Evidence of optional provider consent; contract acknowledgement separately | Verified owner, backend, authorized reviewer | **Proposed** relationship + limitation period approved by counsel; append-only evidence, record withdrawals; retention exception procedure required |
| AI provider requests | Optional third-party business analysis consent; cross-border assessment | Agnes AI or DeepSeek: question scrubbed + allowlisted aggregate values and pseudonym | Provider retention, training usage, processing regions, contract/DPA and deletion assistance **verify actual API contract**. No zero-retention/no-training promise |
| Privacy requests/events | Rights handling, verification/security and legal obligations | Authenticated owner, MFA/RBAC internal reviewer; scoped support needs merchant and justification | **Proposed** completed request + 24 months unless dispute/hold; initial message excludes identity documents/passwords/cards. A deletion request does not auto-delete mandatory ledger or evidence |
| Email reminders, support actions, fingerprints, IP/UA, access logs | Contract communications, abuse prevention and security; optional marketing separately if added | Supabase/Vercel/Resend as configured, authorized support | **Proposed** operational logs 90 days, support 24 months; billing/audit exceptions legal verify; unnecessary fingerprints and precise location must be reviewed |
| Public blog/media/RSS, n8n/ChatGPT editorial automation | Publishing public approved editorial content | Public visitors/crawlers, configured editorial processors | Until editorial removal/rights expiry; backups/cache remove on approved request. Do not use private POS/customer/staff data as generation prompts |
| Backups, object storage, CDN and observability | Reliability, incident recovery | Actual hosting providers/subprocessors | Actual retention, recovery windows, storage ACLs and region **owner/platform verify**. Deletion tombstones must be reapplied on restore; backups expire on approved cycle, not selectively overwritten blindly |

Release blocks: assign data owners; approve durations and bases; validate overseas transfers and Indonesian record storage obligations (observed Supabase region Mumbai); verify processor terms and whether a data protection officer/PSE registration is required.
